type: Changed
title: A bigger Save, and a clearer forecast calendar
---

Save on the daily report is twice as tall — a target a thumb finds one-handed, in the dark. A fever no longer prints a line about being left out of the forecast, the month grid on the Forecast screen is now labelled **Forecast calendar** so it does not share a title with the prediction above it, and its fertile-window and predicted-period dots now clear the day number by the same margin the bleeding droplet always did.
