title: Waking temperature
---

Add your morning temperature to a day's report — a slider across the range one actually lands in, or three digits in the box beside it, which fills in the leading 3 and the decimal point for you. It is optional, and it is the single most useful thing you can give the forecast: the rise after ovulation is what pins down when the next period is due. A fever is recorded on the day it belongs to and left out of the forecast, which cannot read one as a cycle. [Learn more](feature:daily-report)
