title: Lust, sex and fertility tests
---

Three new answers on the daily report, and all three feed the forecast. Lust and sex are one tap each, beside blood and mood swings; a fertility test is optional and records what an ovulation strip said, which is a different claim from not having tested. They are read as a group because they speak about ovulation rather than about the days before a period — and a positive strip dates ovulation to within a day, so it sharpens the next date from the first cycle you use one, without waiting for a pattern to build.
