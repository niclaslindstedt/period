type: Fixed
title: The phone's clock and battery stay readable on every theme
doc: native-app
---

The status bar took its colour from the phone's light or dark setting rather than the app's theme, so a dark theme on a phone in light mode (or a light theme in dark mode) drew the clock and battery in the same shade as the page behind them. The status bar now follows the theme the app shows and switches with it.
