---
type: Changed
title: Rounder corners, clearer dates
---

Dates are now written one way throughout — an abbreviated month, as in **8 Sep** — instead of a long form on some lines and a short one on others; the Forecast screen sets each date in a pill and marks every line with a glyph, the daily report's Save says whether it is writing to this device or to your cloud account, the month grid sits centred on its own screen, and every card, button and control in the app takes a rounder corner.
