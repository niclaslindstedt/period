The iPhone and Android app no longer offers Dropbox: Settings has no Sync section, your reports stay on the phone, and a backup you export through the share sheet is the only way they leave it — a development build that had Dropbox connected comes back to this device with every report still on it, while the website and the desktop app keep Dropbox as before.
