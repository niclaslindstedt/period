The iPhone and Android app no longer offers a cloud copy: Settings has no Sync section, your reports stay on the phone, and a backup you export through the share sheet is the only way they leave it — a development build that had one connected comes back to this device with every report still on it, while the website and the desktop app keep theirs as before. Settings → About and the About screen say so — nothing is sent when the app opens, and nothing goes to iCloud or any other cloud — and link to the full privacy policy. If the device refuses to save, the message points at its storage space.
