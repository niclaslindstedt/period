The phone app no longer offers iCloud Drive. Your reports are health data, and Apple's guidelines keep personal health information out of iCloud, so **Settings → Sync** offers this device and your own Dropbox, and nothing else — the same two places as the website and the desktop app.

The app has not been released on the App Store yet, so there are no reports in anyone's iCloud to move and no migration to run. A development build that had iCloud chosen falls back to this device on its next launch, with every report still on it.
