---
type: Changed
title: The forecast is a distribution, not a date
breaking: true
---

The Forecast screen now shows how likely each day ahead is to be the one your period starts, with 50/80/95% credible bands, from a Bayesian model fitted to your own history. Pick **simple** for the date and the range, or **advanced** for the model's parameters, the patterns it learned, and how well it has done on your past cycles — both show the same prediction. [Learn more](feature:forecast)
