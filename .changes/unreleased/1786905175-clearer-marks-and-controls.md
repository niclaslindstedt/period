A predicted period is now the same rose as the bleeding you reported and a predicted fertile window the same blue as one you are in — outlined rather than a different colour, so the calendar shows two colours and not four; today's number is a size larger in both the month grid and the week row; and the Forecast chart's **Columns** / **Curve** and **Per day** / **Cumulative** chips became pairs you pick from, so what a tap will do is visible instead of guessed.
