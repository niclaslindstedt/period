type: Added
title: A Status screen, and a Calendar of its own
---

The app now opens on **Status**: what today is in one word — _Fertile_, _Not fertile_, _Period_ — with how sure that is as a percentage, and a week of days either side of today. The month grid has left the Forecast screen for a **Calendar** tab of its own, where each day's number now sits on a coloured circle instead of carrying a mark underneath it.
