title: The sources behind the forecast, in the app
---

**Settings → About and sources** lists the studies, guidelines and health service pages the forecast's numbers come from — the cycle and luteal defaults, the fertile window, the temperature rise and the rule that spots it, when mood and desire move in a cycle — each with a line on what it is used for, the words the app took from it, and a link to the source. It works offline; the link is the only thing that leaves the app, and only when you tap it. [Learn more](feature:sources)
