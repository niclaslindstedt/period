# Light and dark

One light theme, one dark theme, or follow the device — set in Settings →
Appearance, applied instantly.

That is the entire theme surface, on purpose. The underlying framework ships a
dozen palettes and a full appearance picker; a tracker that gets fifteen seconds
of attention a day earns nothing from a theme gallery, and every extra palette
is another surface to keep legible. "System" is the default, because the app is
often opened in bed and the OS already knows what that means.

The choice also drives the browser's own chrome: the page ships a
`theme-color` for each scheme, so an installed PWA's status bar matches rather
than sitting as a bright strip above a dark app.
